# Email to the case officer / LEO advisor

Send before publishing the Base44 app or letting testers in. Keep the reply on file.

```
Subject: Question about pre-launch testing – LatherForge

Hi [Name],

While my application is being assessed, I want to make sure I stay within the rules.

LatherForge is not trading: there are no payments and no income, and paid plans are switched off until January 2027.

Could you confirm whether the following are OK before approval:
1. Posting free soap-making tips on TikTok/YouTube and collecting a free waitlist
2. Letting a small group of testers (about 10–20) use the app free of charge to give feedback, with no payment

I won't open it to the public or take any payment until my approval is confirmed.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[PPSN / reference number]
```
