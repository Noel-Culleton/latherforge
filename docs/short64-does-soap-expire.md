## Facts check (safety-critical)

- Cold process soap does not become unsafe with age; it keeps cleaning.
- Over time the unsaponified oils can oxidise: orange spots (DOS, "dreaded orange spots") and a rancid smell. Scent also fades.
- UK/EU cosmetics labels need a "best before end of" date if the product lasts 30 months or less, or an open-jar (PAO) symbol if longer. Not legal advice, check current rules.
