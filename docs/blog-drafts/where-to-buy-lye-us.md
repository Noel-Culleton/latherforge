## What you're looking for

Soap needs **sodium hydroxide (NaOH)**, usually sold as "lye". For bar soap, buy:
- **99%+ pure sodium hydroxide.** Nothing added.
- **Not drain cleaner** unless the label says 100% sodium hydroxide with no additives. Many drain cleaners contain other chemicals. A soap supplier is the safer choice.
- **Beads or microbeads.** Easy to weigh and less dusty than flakes.

(Liquid soap uses potassium hydroxide, KOH. That's a different product.)

## US sellers that sell lye for soap making

- **Certified Lye** sells 99.99% sodium hydroxide microbeads in 20 oz canisters and larger sizes. Its site lists a refund for Handcrafted Soap and Cosmetic Guild members. Source: certified-lye.com
- **Lehman's** sells USA-made lye beads for soap making in 2 lb and 5 lb resealable bags. Source: lehmans.com (lye beads product page)
- **Bulk Apothecary** sells sodium hydroxide and ships it by UPS Ground or freight, because lye is a hazardous material. Source: bulkapothecary.com (sodium hydroxide page)
- **Soap-making supply shops** (for example, the big online soap suppliers) usually stock lye too. It's often cheapest to add it to an order you're placing anyway.

**Shipping:** lye is a hazardous material for shipping, so expect ground shipping only and sometimes a hazmat fee. Many sellers won't ship it outside the US.

## Storing lye safely

- Keep it in the original sealed container, clearly labelled, away from children and pets.
- Close the lid straight away. Lye pulls moisture from the air, and damp beads clump and weigh wrong.
- Store it away from acids and anything aluminium.

## Before you buy: work out how much you need

Lye is the one ingredient you can't guess. Run your recipe through a lye calculator first so you buy the right amount and get the exact weight for every batch.
