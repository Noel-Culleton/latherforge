## Caustic soda is lye

In New Zealand, the lye used for soap is usually sold as **caustic soda**. Chemically it's **sodium hydroxide (NaOH)**. It's the one ingredient every bar of cold process soap needs, and the one you can't swap or guess.

## Where to buy it in NZ

- **Go Native** sells sodium hydroxide as 99% pure fine granules (sometimes called pearls). Its page lists NZ$28.00 and free shipping on orders over $130. Source: gonative.co.nz
- **Pure Nature** sells 1 kg of sodium hydroxide as **pick-up only**. Its page explains that it's a corrosive dangerous good and needs special handling by couriers. Source: purenature.co.nz
- **Bunnings NZ** sells Glitz Caustic Soda 500g, which the listing describes as suitable for soap making, with sodium hydroxide as the active ingredient. Source: bunnings.co.nz

**Why pick-up only?** Caustic soda is classed as a dangerous good for transport. Some sellers don't courier it, others charge extra or take longer. If you're rural, check delivery before you plan a batch around it.

## What to check on the label

- **Sodium hydroxide, 98–99%+.** Nothing else listed.
- **Never a drain-cleaner blend.** If there are other ingredients, it's not for soap.
- **Sealed container.** Caustic soda pulls moisture from the air. Damp granules clump and weigh wrong.
- **Store it safely:** original labelled container, lid on, away from children, pets, acids and aluminium.

## Safety, every time

- Goggles and gloves on before you open it.
- Add caustic soda **to** water, never water to caustic soda.
- Use stainless steel or PP5 plastic jugs. Never aluminium.
