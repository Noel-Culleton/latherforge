## Buying soap supplies in Canada

Most soap-making guides online assume you live in the US. Canadian makers run into two problems: US suppliers often won't ship lye across the border, and cross-border shipping and duty can double the price of oils. The good news is that there are well-established Canadian suppliers that ship lye within Canada.

## Lye (sodium hydroxide)

Lye is classed as a dangerous good for shipping, so most sellers only ship it inside the country and by ground. Expect limits and extra fees on large sizes.

- **Voyageur Soap & Candle** (Surrey, BC) ships sodium hydroxide within Canada by ground service only. Its site lists a dangerous-goods charge on the 55 lb bag; smaller sizes don't carry it.
  Source: voyageursoapandcandle.com/products/lye-sodium-hydroxide
- **Windy Point Soap Making Supplies** limits online lye orders to 8 kg in total within Canada. Its 25 kg bulk lye is pickup only.
  Source: windypointsoap.com/collections/lye

**What to check on any lye you buy:** 99%+ sodium hydroxide (NaOH), no additives (never drain cleaner), and a sealed container. Lye pulls moisture from the air, and damp lye throws your measurements out.

## Oils, butters and fragrance

- **Voyageur Soap & Candle** carries carrier oils, butters, fragrance oils and moulds.
- **New Directions Aromatics** (Ontario) is known for essential oils and fragrance, and also sells base oils.
- **Saffire Blue** (Ontario) sells oils, fragrance and soap bases.

Soap-making forum users have recommended all three for years. Opinions on price and quality vary, and many of those posts are several years old, so compare current prices yourself.

**Tip:** shipping is often charged by weight, so it's usually cheaper to put heavy oils in one larger order than to place several small ones.

## Grocery and warehouse stores

Olive oil, canola oil and coconut oil from grocery and warehouse stores work well for basic recipes. Check the label: you want pure oil, not a blend. Shea butter, cocoa butter and castor oil are usually cheaper from a soap supplier.

## Before you sell in Canada

Soap you sell in Canada is generally treated as a cosmetic, which comes with notification and labelling rules from Health Canada. We cover this in our next guide, *Selling Soap in Canada: Cosmetic Notification and Labels*. Until then, check Health Canada's cosmetics pages directly.
