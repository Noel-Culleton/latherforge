## Full description (max 4000)
The free soap calculator for handmade soap makers. Work out exactly how much lye and water your recipe needs, save your favourite recipes, and know the day every batch is cured and ready to use.

WHAT IT DOES
• Lye calculator for bar soap (NaOH) and liquid soap (KOH, 90% purity adjusted)
• Over 40 oils, butters and waxes: olive, coconut, shea, cocoa butter, castor, lard, tallow, avocado, hemp, babassu, beeswax and more
• Grams or ounces
• Adjustable superfat from 0 to 20%
• Cold process and hot process
• Save recipes on your phone and look them up at the bench
• Cure countdown: tap "I made this today" and see exactly when each batch is ready
• Cost per bar: enter your batch cost and number of bars
• Works offline once installed, perfect for the workshop

MADE FOR SOAP MAKERS
Whether you are making your first batch of cold process soap or running a small soap business, LatherForge gives you the numbers you need in seconds, without spreadsheets or desktop-only calculators.

SELLING YOUR SOAP?
The Selling tab previews what's coming in LatherForge, the business platform for soap makers: pricing and profit, printable ingredient labels, and batch records for traceability. Register for early access from inside the app.

SAFETY
Lye is caustic. Always wear gloves and eye protection, add lye to water (never water to lye) and double-check any recipe before you make it.

Free soap recipes, a SAP value chart and soap making guides at latherforge.com.
