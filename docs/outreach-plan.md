# Soap business outreach plan: follow → warm up → email

**Goal:** 300–500 real soap businesses in Canada, Australia, the UK, New Zealand, Ireland and the US know who LatherForge is before launch, and ~10–20% try it.
**Tracker:** `outreach/soap-business-tracker.csv` (open in Excel or Google Sheets; one row per business).

## Stage 1: Find and follow (now – end of November)
**Daily, ~20 minutes, from the LatherForge Facebook Page** (not your personal profile):
1. Search Facebook for these terms, filtered to **Pages**:
   - "handmade soap" + country/city (e.g. "handmade soap Toronto", "soap Melbourne", "soap Auckland", "artisan soap Dublin", "soap company Manchester")
   - "cold process soap", "natural soap company", "soap studio", "soapery", "skincare small batch"
2. Pick **real selling businesses**: posts in the last month, sells online or at markets, has a website or shop. Skip hobbyists with no shop.
3. **Follow 20–25 a day, max.** Following hundreds in one go can get the Page restricted.
4. Add each one to the tracker: name, country, Facebook link, website.

Where else to find them: Etsy shop "about" pages, craft-fair stallholder lists, the LatherForge TikTok and Instagram followers who run soap businesses. Facebook groups are fine for *finding* names, but never post promotions or DM people from groups.

## Stage 2: Warm up (ongoing, 1–2 weeks per business before any email)
- Like a couple of their posts and leave **one genuine comment** (a real compliment or question about the soap, not about LatherForge).
- Note the date and what you did in "Engaged".
- This makes the later email feel familiar instead of cold.

## Stage 3: Get business details (as you go)
From their **website** (contact page or footer) or Facebook Page "About":
- Business email (not a personal gmail/hotmail unless it's clearly their business address)
- Owner's first name if shown
- One product they sell (for the personal line)
No email listed, only a contact form? Note "form only". You can send a short version through the form yourself.

**Or just send me the websites.** Paste them in chat, or share the sheet, and I'll fill in the email, name and product for you.

## Stage 4: Email (two waves)
| Wave | When | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–10 December | Early access + founding-member price lock for January subscribers |
| 2 | 4–15 January | "We're live" + 14-day free trial (the New Year "gift to yourself" angle) |

- Avoid mid-November to Christmas: soap makers are flat out with markets.
- I write each email personally (their name and product), put it in your Gmail as a **draft**, and you check and send. **10–20 a day.**
- Send from a LatherForge address (latherforge@zohomail.eu, or hello@latherforge.com once set up), not a personal Gmail.
- Every email: who you are, why you're writing to *their* business, one link, "reply STOP and I won't email again". Stop immediately if anyone says no. Business addresses only.
- Canada/Australia: only email addresses they publish publicly, and never if their site says "no marketing emails". Not legal advice.

## Stage 5: Follow-up
- One polite follow-up after 7–10 days if no reply. Then stop.
- Anyone who signs up: welcome email + ask for feedback (early users become testimonials).
- Weekly: count followed / emailed / replied / signed up. I'll add it to the Monday report if you share the sheet.
