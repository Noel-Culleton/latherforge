# January 2027 Meta ads estimate (one month per country)

**Estimates only.** There is no live Meta ad account data yet. Figures use typical Meta benchmarks for small SaaS/hobby audiences. Replace them with real numbers after the first 2 weeks of spend.

## Assumptions
- Campaign: one month, 1–31 Jan 2027. Objective: 14-day trial sign-ups (lead/registration event), plus retargeting of site visitors.
- January timing is good: soap searches peak Jan–Mar, and ad prices usually drop after the Christmas rush.
- Link click to trial sign-up: about 4% (range 2–6%).
- Trial to paying: about 15% (range 10–25%). Paying customers mostly land in February, after the 14-day trial.
- Average plan value: about €35/month (mostly Craft Pack, some Business Pack).

## United States: not promoted (decided 8 Oct)
No paid ads in the US in January. US makers can still find the site and buy in USD. The US is about 71% of English-language soap-calculator searches, so this budget reaches a much smaller market.

## What €1,450 likely buys
- About 9–10 paying customers, roughly €330/month in recurring revenue.
- Cost per paying customer: about €150. That pays back after about 4–5 months if customers stay.
- Plus about 60 trial users and a retargeting audience for February.

## Lean option: €850
Canada €450 + Australia €400. The two strongest markets per head. Add the UK, NZ and Ireland in February if January costs come in on target.

## Warnings
- Small budgets (€5–10/day) in NZ, Ireland and UK give Meta too little data to learn. Group them into one "UK + IE + NZ" ad set instead of separate ones.
- Must be ready before spending: Meta Pixel (or Conversions API) + cookie banner, Stripe live, trial terms matching the ads exactly, and the grant officer's OK.
- Prices in ads must state amount, currency, billing period and renewal terms together.
- Review after 7 and 14 days: stop any country above €2.50 per click or €60 per trial sign-up.
