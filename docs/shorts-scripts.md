# Short video scripts (YouTube Shorts / Reels / TikTok)

Each is 20–40 seconds. Film hands at the bench plus a screen recording of the calculator.

## 1. How much lye for 1 kg of oils?
- HOOK (0–3s): "How much lye do you need for a kilo of oils? It's not one number."
- (3–15s) "Every oil needs a different amount. Coconut oil needs about 35% more lye than olive oil."
- (15–28s) Screen: enter 700 g olive, 300 g coconut, 5% superfat. "For this recipe: 141.9 grams of lye."
- (28–35s) "Change one oil and the number changes. Always run it through a calculator."

## 2. The one mistake that ruins soap
- HOOK: "This soap looks fine but it would burn your skin."
- "Too much lye. It happens when you swap an oil and don't recalculate."
- Screen: swap coconut for palm kernel oil, show the lye amount change.
- "Recalculate every time you change a recipe."
- CTA: calculator link.

## 3. What is superfat?
- HOOK: "Why do soap makers use less lye than the maths says?"
- "It's called superfat. Leave around 5% of the oils unreacted and you get a milder bar, plus a safety margin."
- Screen: slide superfat from 0% to 5% to 20% and show the lye dropping.
- "Salt bars use 20%. Most bars use 5%."
- CTA: calculator link.

## 4. How long does soap really need to cure?
- HOOK: "Your soap is safe after 2 days, so why wait 6 weeks?"
- "Curing is about water. Fresh soap is up to 30% water. Cure it and it gets harder, milder and lasts twice as long."
- Show bars on a rack. "Weigh one bar each week. When the weight stops dropping, it's ready."
- Screen: app cure countdown.
- CTA: "The free LatherForge app counts down every batch."
