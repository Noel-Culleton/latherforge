# New Year campaign: "The one present you forgot to give yourself"

**Idea (Noel, 8 Oct):** speak to the soap maker at the end of a big year. You made the soap, put in the hours, wrote everything down, and you're ready for next year. But did you give *yourself* a present? LatherForge is the one gift that makes your life easier and helps the business grow.

**Who:** working soap makers and small soap businesses, not hobby beginners. Canada, Australia, UK, New Zealand, Ireland (paid). The US is organic only.
**When:** 26 Dec – 31 Jan. Teaser 26–31 Dec ("did you give yourself a present?"), launch 1 Jan, push 2–31 Jan. January is peak soap-search month.
**Goal:** 14-day trial sign-ups at launch (pre-launch: early-access sign-ups).

## Direct outreach (Noel, by hand)
Message soap businesses directly: their Facebook Page inbox or the business email on their website.
- Keep it **personal and low volume** (10–20 a day). Use their name, mention one of their products, and give one line on what LatherForge does with a link. No copy-paste blasts. Facebook restricts accounts that send bulk unsolicited messages.
- **Email rules:** in Ireland and the UK, emailing a *business* address about something relevant to their business is generally allowed if you say who you are and include an easy opt-out. Don't email personal addresses (gmail etc.) cold. Not legal advice.
- Template (draft):
  > Hi [name], I love your [product]. I'm Noel, an Irish soap maker building LatherForge, a tool that keeps recipes, batches, cost per bar and labels in one place for soap businesses like yours. It launches in January with a 14-day free trial. Would you like a look? [link]. If it's not for you, just say and I won't message again.

## Must be true before any ad runs
- Trial terms final: the ads say "14-day free trial", so the site, checkout and terms must match exactly (card needed or not, what happens after).
- Any price shown must have amount, currency, billing period and renewal terms together.
- Meta Pixel (or Conversions API) and the cookie banner live; the landing page matches the ad.
- Grant officer's written OK before taking payments.
- No promises we can't back: no "grow your sales by X%", no invented reviews or customer numbers.
