Vegan Palm-Free Soap Recipe

A vegan, palm-free cold process soap recipe using cocoa butter and shea butter for hardness.

BeginnerCold process5% superfatCure: 5 weeks

Why this recipe works

Cocoa and shea butter replace palm oil for hardness, so you get a firm vegan bar without palm or animal fats.

Ingredients

Ingredient%GramsOunces
Olive Oil40%400 g12.8 oz
Coconut Oil (76°)25%250 g8 oz
Cocoa Butter15%150 g4.8 oz
Shea Butter10%100 g3.2 oz
Avocado Oil5%50 g1.6 oz
Castor Oil5%50 g1.6 oz
Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)139 g4.4 oz
Distilled water278 g8.9 oz

Amounts are for 1000 g (or 32 oz) of oils, a batch of about 1417 g before curing. Water is 2:1 to lye by weight.

Resize in the soap calculator

Method

  1. Put on gloves and eye protection and clear the work area.
  2. Weigh the water (or frozen liquid) into a heat-safe jug. Weigh the lye separately, then slowly add the lye to the water, never the other way round. Stir until dissolved and leave to cool.
  3. Weigh and melt the hard oils and butters, then add the liquid oils.
  4. When the oils and lye solution are both around 35–45°C (unless the tips say otherwise), pour the lye solution into the oils.
  5. Stick blend in short bursts until light trace (like thin custard). Stir in fragrance, colour and any additives.
  6. Pour into the mould, cover and leave for 24–48 hours.
  7. Unmould, cut into bars and cure on a rack in a dry, airy place.

Tips

  • Use deodorised cocoa butter if you do not want a chocolate scent.
  • Cocoa butter speeds trace a little; soap at 35–40°C.

⚠️ Lye safety

Lye is caustic. Wear gloves and eye protection, work in a ventilated room, keep children and pets away, and always add lye to water. Always run any recipe through a lye calculator before making it, especially if you change an oil.
